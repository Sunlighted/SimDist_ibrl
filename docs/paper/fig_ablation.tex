\caption{%
  \textbf{What the two SimADEPT modifications are worth, and what the tether has
  to be.}
  \textbf{(a)} Both modifications help and neither is sufficient: removing the
  tether costs $7.4$ points, removing the ensemble $11.7$, removing both $20.7$.
  On the additive scale the two look independent, but the arms differ in
  headroom --- the tether closes $34\%$ of the remaining gap on the single-head
  base and $57\%$ on the ensemble base, so the two modifications are
  complementary, not merely stacked. The seed spread also collapses
  monotonically as components are added ($\pm10.1 \to \pm1.1$).
  \textbf{(b)} Every variant is first \emph{calibrated} on stage-2 alone: its
  strength is swept until it reaches the same proximity to the source policy,
  $D_{\mathrm{src}} = \mathbb{E}_{s \sim \mathcal{D}^d_s}\lVert
  \pi_\theta(s)-\pi_s(s)\rVert_1 / d_a$, as the deployed setting. Without this
  step a comparison of \emph{forms} is confounded by \emph{strength}. Only then
  is stage~3 run. In the strength column the shape parameter ($\tau$,
  $\epsilon$) is fixed by design and $\lambda$ is the quantity the sweep
  sets, which is why it spans four orders of magnitude; a row showing a
  single entry has no shape parameter, and weight decay is written
  $\mathrm{wd}$ because its attractor is $\theta{=}0$ rather than $\pi_s$,
  so it is not a $\lambda$ in the sense of Eq.~\ref{eq:tether}.
  Matching $D_{\mathrm{src}}$ is what makes rows (i)
  comparable, and the two rows that cannot reach the working point at any
  strength --- weight decay ($0.209$) and the recorded dataset action
  ($0.154$) --- are reported at their attainable floor and fail for that reason.
  The identity of the divergence is almost free (the hinge matches ours,
  $-0.3$, paired $t{=}0.3$; the saturating penalty is if anything slightly
  ahead, $+1.7$, $t{=}2.4$), but the \emph{metric} is not (parameter-space
  $L_2$, $-22.9$, $t{=}10.3$) and neither is the target (recorded actions,
  $-19.4$, $t{=}9.1$). Restricting the anchor to expert\,$\wedge$\,success
  states is neutral ($-0.6$, $t{=}0.4$) even though it makes the recorded
  actions $7\times$ closer to $\pi_s$: the better action label is paid for
  with state coverage.
  Threading, $100$ evaluation episodes per point, $\pm$ one seed s.d.; all
  deltas are paired within seed, and a seed is a joint draw of the training
  seed and of the $20$ target demonstrations.
  $^{\dagger}$ from the calibration sweep; this arm predates the in-run
  $D_{\mathrm{src}}$ probe.
